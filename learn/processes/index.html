Understand Linux processes

A process is a running instance of a program. Learn to inspect processes, understand parent/child relationships, and use signals without treating process management as guesswork.

Inspect first

ps
ps aux
top

ps gives a snapshot, while top presents a live view of resource use. The exact columns differ by platform.

Foreground and background jobs

command &
jobs
fg

A trailing & starts a command asynchronously in shells that support job control. jobs lists shell jobs and fg brings a job to the foreground.

Signals and safe control

kill -TERM PID
ps -p PID

TERM asks a process to terminate cleanly. Prefer a graceful signal and verify the process before acting. Do not blindly copy a PID from an unrelated example.

Important: Process-management commands affect the operating system outside the browser simulator. Use real-system examples only on systems you administer.
