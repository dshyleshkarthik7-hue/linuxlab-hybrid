What we collect

Account, assessment, certificate and service data is collected only where required to provide the requested feature. Learning progress is designed to avoid unnecessary date-of-birth collection.

Signed-in features may process account identifiers and learning or assessment progress. Verified assessments require server-side attempt state and grading.

AI Tutor

The Tutor processes the question and limited learning context needed to answer it. Tutor requests require authentication and are rate limited. Secrets and provider credentials remain server-side.

Security logs and service records may be retained as necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service. Assessment and certificate records are retained according to the service's operational and credential requirements.

Third parties

The service uses infrastructure providers for hosting, identity, storage, database services and AI inference. Their processing is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Your choices
