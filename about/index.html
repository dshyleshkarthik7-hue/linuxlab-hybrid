  • Linux and ROS 2 for voice AI, computer vision, OpenCV/MediaPipe and edge reasoning.

LinuxTerminal

LinuxTerminal is a learning platform combining a safe browser simulator, a real browser Linux emulator, a 200-command curriculum, 50 dedicated command tutorial pages, 500 quiz questions, 100 practice challenges, ISO integrity verification and a contextual free open-model AI Tutor.

The real VM profiles use pinned Alpine Linux and Linux 4 artifacts. The verified loader downloads the complete image and checks its SHA-256 digest before the emulator consumes it.
