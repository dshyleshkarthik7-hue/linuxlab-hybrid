head

Show the Beginning of Files

Prints the first part of one or more files or streams.

💡 Definition

head previews the beginning of files or streams.

Use -n to select how many lines to display.

⌘ Syntax

head [options] [file...]
  • head -n 5 access.log
  • head --help
  • man head

▶ Quick Use

  1. Parse inputRead options and input files.
  2. Open inputRead the requested stream or file.
  3. Select prefixKeep the requested initial records.
  4. OutputWrite selected content to stdout.
