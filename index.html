What is Linux, and how can you try it?

Linux is an operating system—the main software that helps a computer run, similar to Windows or macOS. You do not have to erase or change your computer to start learning it.

🔀 Dual Boot

Install Linux beside your current OS. Back up first and follow your distribution's official installation guide.

Minimum comfortable starting point: 4 GB RAM and about 25 GB free storage. For a smoother desktop experience, 8 GB RAM and 50 GB+ free storage are recommended.
STAGE 01

Download ISO Image

Choose a distribution and download its ISO from the official website. Keep the ISO somewhere easy to find.

STAGE 02

Flash to USB Drive

For a virtual machine, attach the ISO inside VirtualBox. For installation on a physical computer, use a trusted bootable-USB tool and follow the distribution documentation.

STAGE 03

UEFI & Partitioning

Before changing partitions, back up important files. Read the installer carefully and keep your existing operating system if you want dual boot.

STAGE 04

Bootloader & Users

Finish the installer, create your user account and restart. Keep learning commands in LinuxLab while you get comfortable.
